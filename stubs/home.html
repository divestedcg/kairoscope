About¶

I re-implemented the below mental health screeners as I found others were not adequately private or secure. I kept them true to their source as much as possible. They are largely geared towards adults and are likely not suitable for children. Results are outputted in a consistent single-line format to be saved in your notes app of choice. All tests are the sole ownership of their respective authors noted on each linked page. All tests have been verified for accuracy against their sources.

Policy:

  • 100% client side
  • zero of the following: accounts, cookies, ads, trackers, referrals, unnecessary scripts, network calls
  • Tor Browser is explicitly exempted and encouraged
  • FOSS & contributions welcomed - source code (AGPL-3.0) available via: Codeberg, GitHub, GitLab

Disclaimer #1: These are not suitable for diagnosis, but are useful to determine if it is worth pursuing a professional opinion/assessment/treatment. Some can also be used to track your progression over time.

Disclaimer #2: I am not a mental health professional, just someone who has always found it interesting.

If you are currently in crisis please see the following resource list from HelpGuide.org.

Status: Twenty-two tests are currently available, with ~five more planned.

Psychosis¶

Do you experience sensations (such as sights or sounds) or (unusual) beliefs which don't align with your past reality?

Note: Primary psychosis is a core component of schizophrenia, but is also central to other conditions such as schizoaffective disorder and mood disorders with psychotic feature modifiers. Secondary psychosis is caused by other interactions such as physical conditions. Lastly there is also substance induced psychosis.

Read more about psychosis on: Wikipedia

Read more about schizophrenia on: Wikipedia, SAMHSA.gov, NIH.gov, NHS.uk, HelpGuide.org

Read more about schizoaffective disorder on: Wikipedia, HelpGuide.org
